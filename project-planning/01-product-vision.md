# CampusLore - Product Vision

## 1. Project Overview

CampusLore is an AI-powered campus knowledge platform that enables university students to share, discover, and preserve valuable experiences across academic and campus life. The platform aims to bridge the knowledge gap between different student batches by creating a structured repository of experiences related to academics, internships, placements, research, clubs, and campus life.

Unlike traditional social platforms where information is quickly buried in conversations, CampusLore focuses on creating long-lasting, searchable knowledge that benefits future students.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

A significant amount of valuable knowledge is lost every academic year as senior students graduate.

Important information such as:

* Course preparation strategies
* Internship and placement experiences
* Professor-specific advice
* Club and society experiences
* Research opportunities
* Campus life tips

is often scattered across WhatsApp groups, Discord servers, personal conversations, or individual notes. This information becomes difficult to access, impossible to search effectively, and is rarely preserved for future batches.

As a result, juniors frequently repeat the same mistakes and struggle to find reliable guidance despite the knowledge already existing within the student community.

---

## 3. Proposed Solution

CampusLore provides a centralized platform where verified university students can contribute and access structured knowledge based on real experiences.

The platform allows students to:

* Share detailed experiences and guidance.
* Organize content into structured categories.
* Discover relevant experiences through intelligent search.
* Bookmark valuable resources.
* Participate in discussions through comments.
* Receive AI-assisted summaries and recommendations.

By combining community-generated knowledge with AI-powered organization and search, CampusLore transforms scattered student experiences into an accessible institutional knowledge base.

---

## 4. Target Users

### Primary Users

* Undergraduate students
* Senior students sharing experiences
* Junior students seeking guidance

### Secondary Users

* Student clubs and organizations
* Academic communities
* Research groups
* Campus administrators (future scope)

---

## 5. Core Objectives

* Preserve valuable student knowledge across batches.
* Make campus information easily searchable and accessible.
* Encourage meaningful knowledge sharing within the university.
* Reduce repetitive questions by building a structured knowledge repository.
* Enhance knowledge discovery using AI-powered features.
* Maintain a trusted and verified student community.

---

---

## 7. Product Principles

CampusLore is designed around the following principles:

### Knowledge First

The platform prioritizes preserving useful information over social engagement.

### Verified Community

Only verified university students can contribute, ensuring authenticity and trust.

### Structured Information

Knowledge should remain organized, searchable, and useful long after it is published.

### AI as an Assistant

Artificial Intelligence enhances organization, discovery, and summarization without replacing authentic student experiences.

### Long-Term Value

Every feature should contribute to building a lasting knowledge repository for future students.

---

## 8. Initial Deployment

CampusLore is designed as a university-agnostic platform capable of supporting multiple institutions.

However, the first deployment will be limited to students of **Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT), Jaipur**, with authentication restricted to verified **@mnit.ac.in** email addresses.

The architecture will remain extensible to support additional universities in future releases.

---

## 9. Success Metrics

The success of CampusLore will be evaluated using metrics such as:

* Number of verified student registrations
* Number of published knowledge posts
* Search engagement
* Bookmark activity
* User retention
* Community contributions across categories
* AI feature adoption (future versions)

---

## 10. Vision Statement

CampusLore aims to become the institutional memory of a university.

By preserving student experiences and making them easily discoverable, the platform ensures that every graduating batch leaves behind valuable knowledge for the next generation.

Instead of knowledge disappearing with each graduating class, CampusLore transforms it into a continuously growing resource that empowers students throughout their academic journey.
